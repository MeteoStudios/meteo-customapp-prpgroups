# meteo-customapp-prpgroups

A Groups app for [Meteo Phone](https://docs.meteofivem.net/paid-scripts/meteo-phone) that integrates with [prp-bridge](https://github.com/ProdigyPRP/prp-bridge), the official bridge for Prodigy Studios scripts. Lets players create a group, view members and their online status, toggle invites, kick members, and leave or disband - all from inside the phone.

## Install

1. Drop the resource into your `resources` folder.
2. `ensure meteo-customapp-prpgroups` after `meteo-phone` and `prp-bridge`.
3. Open the phone in-game - the **Groups** app shows up in the App Store and can be installed by the player.

## Requirements

- [Meteo Phone](https://docs.meteofivem.net/paid-scripts/meteo-phone)
- [prp-bridge](https://github.com/ProdigyPRP/prp-bridge)
- `ox_lib`
